## Context

Trashumar Ediciones is an independent, nomadic and federal publishing house. The project needed its own platform to showcase its catalog, give visibility to its projects and make it easier for people in its community to connect.

## Challenge

Build an experience that brings editorial content and management tools together in one website, while addressing the different needs of readers, authors, contributors and distributors.

## My contribution

I was responsible for frontend design and development, from the initial structure to the implementation of the main features.

- **Authentication and profiles:** sign-in integration and user profile management.
- **Catalog and projects:** interfaces to showcase books and editorial projects.
- **Community and distributors:** profiles with public information, contact details and distribution areas.
- **Maps:** visualization of distribution areas using Leaflet and location tools.
- **Forms:** form validation and handling with Formik and Yup, alongside external service integrations.
- **Responsive interface:** components and layouts adapted to different screen sizes.

## Technical decisions

- Used **React and Vite** to build the interface and organize the project into components.
- Integrated **Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage** for authentication, data persistence and file storage.
- Used **Material UI** with a custom theme to maintain visual consistency.
- Added focused tools such as **Formik, Yup, Swiper and Leaflet** for forms, galleries and geographic features.

## Current status

The first frontend development stage and the existing Firebase integrations provide the platform's functional foundation. I am currently working on building the backend to continue its development and complete the next stage of the project.

Development is ongoing, so the status shown here reflects a project in progress rather than a finished, closed release.

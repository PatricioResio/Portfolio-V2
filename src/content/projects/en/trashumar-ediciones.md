## Context

Trashumar is a nomadic, federal and independent publisher. It needed a platform to present its catalog and receive projects from authors.

## What I did

- Authentication and user profiles.
- Book catalog and project management.
- **Firebase** integration for authentication, database and storage.
- Responsive components and performance improvements, including image loading.

## Current status

Development is done. The launch is pending resolving the project's billing on Firebase: since 2026, Cloud Storage requires a linked paid plan.

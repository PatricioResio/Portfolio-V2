## Context

An inn in Salta needed its own website to present itself and receive inquiries from travelers from different countries. I built it together with AleMot94.

## The challenge

A single site had to work well on phones and in four languages, with none of them feeling like a second-class translation.

## What I did

- **Mobile-first** design and development of the landing page.
- Gallery, room views, availability and contact.
- The full experience in **four languages**.
